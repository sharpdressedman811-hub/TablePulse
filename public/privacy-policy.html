TablePulse Monitor
Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 1, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to TablePulse Monitor. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information with transparency and care. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, with whom we share it, and the choices you have regarding your data.

By downloading, installing, or using the TablePulse Monitor application ("App"), you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these practices, please do not use the App.

Throughout this policy, "we," "us," and "our" refer to TablePulse Monitor. "You" and "your" refer to the individual or entity using our App.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you use TablePulse Monitor:

Account Information

  • Email address — collected when you create an account or sign in. Used to identify your account, send important notices, and authenticate your sessions.

Restaurant Business Data

  • Revenue figures — daily, weekly, or period-based revenue data you enter or import into the App.
  • Labor costs — staffing and labor expense data associated with your restaurant operations.
  • Covers (guest counts) — the number of guests served during tracked periods.
  • Average check values — per-guest or per-table average spend metrics.

This business data is entered by you and is used solely to power the analytics, dashboards, and insights within the App.

Device and Notification Data

  • Push notification tokens — device tokens generated by your operating system and registered with our push notification provider (OneSignal) when you grant notification permissions. These are used exclusively to deliver push notifications to your device.

App Usage Analytics

  • Aggregated, anonymized data about how you interact with the App — such as features accessed, session duration, and navigation patterns. This data helps us improve the App's performance and user experience. It does not include your business data or personally identifiable information in identifiable form.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Providing the Service — to operate, maintain, and deliver the features of TablePulse Monitor, including storing and displaying your restaurant metrics and analytics.
  • Account Management — to create and manage your account, authenticate your identity, and maintain the security of your session.
  • Push Notifications — to send you alerts, reminders, and updates related to your restaurant data and App activity, where you have granted permission.
  • Subscription Management — to process and manage your subscription, including verifying entitlements and handling billing through our subscription provider.
  • Product Improvement — to analyze aggregated usage patterns and improve the App's features, performance, and user experience.
  • Communications — to send you important notices about your account, updates to this Privacy Policy, or changes to our Terms of Service.
  • Legal Compliance — to comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for their marketing or commercial purposes — ever.

We share data only with the following trusted third-party service providers, and only to the extent necessary to operate the App:

  • Supabase Database and authentication infrastructure. Your account information and restaurant business data are stored securely in Supabase-managed databases. Supabase processes data on our behalf and is contractually prohibited from using your data for any other purpose.
  • OneSignal Push notification delivery. Your device's push notification token is shared with OneSignal solely to deliver notifications you have opted into. OneSignal does not receive your business data or financial metrics.
  • RevenueCat Subscription and in-app purchase management. RevenueCat receives your user identifier and subscription transaction data to verify and manage your subscription entitlements. RevenueCat does not receive your restaurant business data.

We may also disclose your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Legal requirements — if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Protection of rights — to protect the rights, property, or safety of TablePulse Monitor, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of our assets, in which case your data would be transferred subject to the same privacy protections described in this policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information and restaurant business data for as long as your account remains active. This allows us to provide you with continuous access to your historical analytics and metrics.

If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days of receiving and verifying your request. Certain anonymized, aggregated data that cannot be linked back to you may be retained for analytical purposes beyond this period.

We may retain certain information for longer periods where required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement of our agreements.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Access — the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — the right to request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — the right to request that we delete your personal data. You may submit a deletion request by emailing us at privacy@tablepulse.app. We will process your request within 30 days.
  • Portability — the right to request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — the right to object to certain types of processing, including processing for direct marketing purposes.
  • Withdrawal of Consent — where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@tablepulse.app. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

You may also manage push notification permissions at any time through your device's operating system settings or within the App's notification preferences screen.

7. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. TablePulse Monitor employs industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption in transit — all data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security).
  • Encryption at rest — your data stored in our databases is encrypted at rest using AES-256 or equivalent industry-standard encryption.
  • Access controls — access to your data is restricted to authorized personnel and systems on a need-to-know basis.
  • Secure authentication — account access is protected through secure authentication mechanisms managed by Supabase.

While we implement these safeguards, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to continuously improving our security practices to protect your information.

If you believe your account has been compromised or you have identified a security vulnerability, please contact us immediately at privacy@tablepulse.app.

8. Children's Privacy

TablePulse Monitor is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take prompt steps to delete that information from our systems. If you believe we may have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us at privacy@tablepulse.app.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you through one or both of the following methods:

  • Email notification — we will send a notice to the email address associated with your account.
  • In-app notice — we will display a prominent notice within the App when you next open it.

The updated policy will include a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

Your continued use of TablePulse Monitor after the effective date of any updated Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of the revised terms.
